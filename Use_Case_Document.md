## Use Case Document: *Custom GPT*
## GPT Name: 
*HR Support Assistant*
## Problem Statement
- Employees frequently spend valuable time seeking clarification on routine internal guidelines, resulting in a high volume of repetitive queries concerning leave requests, dress code rules, and notice period policies. This constant influx strains the HR support team and delays timely resolutions for staff.

## Solution
- A trained AI assistant powered by official HR policy documents can instantly and accurately answer repetitive employee questions 24/7. This solution reduces the manual workload on HR personnel while empowering employees with immediate, reliable self-service information.

## Target Users
* Employees and staff members seeking instant policy clarifications
* HR support and operations teams managing internal inquiries

## Expected Outcomes
* **Reduced HR Workload:** Decreases the volume of repetitive manual tickets, allowing HR teams to focus on strategic initiatives.
* **Instant Resolutions:** Provides employees with immediate, 24/7 access to accurate policy information without wait times.
* **Improved Consistency:** Ensures uniform and policy-compliant answers are delivered across all internal inquiries.
